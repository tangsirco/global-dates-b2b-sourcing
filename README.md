## Our Products

We focus on four main product categories:

### 1. Dates
Dates for bulk sourcing and potential industrial food processing applications.

### 2. Date Paste
Date paste for potential applications in bakery products, confectionery, energy bars, protein bars, and healthy snacks.

### 3. Date Syrup
Date syrup for potential applications in beverages, confectionery, bakery products, and food manufacturing.

### 4. Date Sugar
Date sugar for potential applications in selected food formulations and industrial food manufacturing.

Product specifications, availability, and commercial terms are subject to supplier confirmation.
