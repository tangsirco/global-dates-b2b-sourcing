Buyer Qualification Questions

Before commercial outreach, research should determine:

- Does the company manufacture or process relevant products?
- Does it use locally produced dates or imported ingredients?
- Is there a genuine sourcing gap?
- Which date ingredient may be technically relevant?
- What product format, origin, or specification is required?
- Does the company import ingredients directly?
- Does it work through an importer or distributor?
- What technical specifications are required?
- What packaging formats are accepted?
- What trial quantity may be appropriate?
- Which certifications or declarations are required?
- What is the correct public business contact channel?
