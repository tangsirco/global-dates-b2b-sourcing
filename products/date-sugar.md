## Product Overview

Date sugar is generally produced from dried dates that are processed and milled into particles or powder. Unlike fully dissolved liquid sweeteners, date sugar may retain fruit solids and may not dissolve completely in every formulation.

Its suitability must therefore be evaluated according to the intended application, required particle size, processing conditions, and finished-product characteristics.

## Industrial Applications

- Cakes and pastries
- Breakfast cereals

- Protein bars
- Healthy snack formulations
- Dry ingredient blends
- Plant-based food products
- Confectionery applications
- Food ingredient preparations

Suitability depends on the verified specification and the manufacturer’s formulation and processing requirements.

## Potential Manufacturing Uses

Food manufacturers may evaluate date sugar as:

- A dry date-based ingredient
- A source of date flavour and colour
- A component in bakery formulations
- An ingredient in cereal and granola blends
- A component in energy and protein bars
- A topping or inclusion for suitable products
- A component in dry food-ingredient blends
- A partial sweetening ingredient where technically appropriate

These are potential applications and do not guarantee dissolution, sweetness equivalence, processing performance, or compatibility with a particular formulation.
