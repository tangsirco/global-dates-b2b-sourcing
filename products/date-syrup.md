# Date Syrup for Food Manufacturing

Tangsir Daryanavard Co. supports international sourcing inquiries for date syrup intended for industrial food and beverage manufacturing.

> **Important:** Product availability, composition, origin, technical specifications, packaging, certifications, pricing, and commercial terms must be confirmed for each sourcing inquiry.

## Industrial Applications

Date syrup may be evaluated for use in:

- Bakery products
- Biscuits and cookies
- Cakes and pastries
- Chocolate and confectionery
- Energy bars and protein bars
- Breakfast cereals and granola
- Beverages and smoothies
- Sauces and dressings
- Dairy or dairy-alternative products
- Plant-based food products
- Healthy snack formulations
- Food ingredient preparations

Suitability depends on the product specification, formulation requirements, processing conditions, and applicable food regulations.

## Potential Manufacturing Uses

Food manufacturers may evaluate date syrup as:

- A liquid sweetening ingredient
- A source of date flavour and colour
- A binder in suitable snack formulations
- A component in bakery and confectionery recipes
- An ingredient in beverage formulations
- A topping or coating component
- A component in sauces or fruit preparations
- A partial replacement for other sweetening ingredients where technically suitable

These are potential applications and do not guarantee performance in a specific product, recipe, or production process.

## Technical Information Buyers Should Request

Before purchasing date syrup for food manufacturing, buyers should request and evaluate:

| Technical area | Information to request |
|---|---|
| Product identity | Product name, origin and raw-material information |
| Composition | Ingredient declaration and information about added ingredients |
| Concentration | Soluble solids or Brix information |
| Physical properties | Colour, flavour, viscosity and consistency |
| Chemical parameters | Moisture, pH and other relevant analytical information |
| Food safety | Microbiological parameters and available test reports |
| Allergens | Allergen statement and cross-contact information |
| Processing | Filtration, concentration and thermal-processing information |
| Storage | Recommended storage conditions and shelf life |
| Packaging | Container type, net weight and pallet configuration |
| Traceability | Batch identification and available traceability documentation |
| Compliance | Available certifications and regulatory documentation |
| Logistics | Minimum order quantity, lead time and shipping conditions |

No technical value, certification, food-safety status, or regulatory compliance should be assumed until supporting documentation has been received and verified for the specific product.
