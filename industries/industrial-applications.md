Overview

Dates and date-derived ingredients may be evaluated for a wide range of industrial food-manufacturing applications.

This document covers:

- Bulk Dates
- Industrial Date Paste
- Date Syrup for Food Manufacturing
- Date Sugar for the Food Industry

The applications described below are potential uses. Actual suitability depends on the exact product, formulation, processing conditions, equipment, target shelf life, required specifications, and destination-market regulations.

No technical performance, specification, certification, or product availability is guaranteed by this document.

Date Sugar

Potential Industrial Functions

Date sugar may be evaluated as:

- A dry fruit-based ingredient
- A component in selected dry blends
- A flavor and color contributor
- An ingredient for selected bakery formulations
- A topping or inclusion
- A component in healthy snack products

Date sugar should not automatically be assumed to perform like fully soluble conventional sugar.

Potential Manufacturing Uses

- Selected bakery formulations
- Biscuits and cookies

Manufacturing Function Matrix

Manufacturing function| Potential date ingredients| Important evaluation points
Binding and cohesion| Date paste, date syrup| Hold, cutting, stickiness, moisture, water activity, and shelf life
Filling and layering| Date paste, date syrup| Pumpability, bake stability, viscosity, flavor, and migration
Fruit inclusions| Whole, pitted, chopped, or diced dates| Size, distribution, moisture, texture, and process tolerance
Liquid flavor or sweetness| Date syrup| Brix, viscosity, color, flavor, solubility, and stability
Dry fruit-based ingredient| Date sugar| Particle size, flow, dispersibility, browning, and texture
Further ingredient processing| Dates, paste, syrup, and sugar| Yield, consistency, traceability, packaging, and economics
