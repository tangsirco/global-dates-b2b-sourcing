## Buyer Research Methodology

For each target country:

1. Identify real food manufacturing companies.
2. Review official company websites.
3. Research relevant product categories and industrial applications.
4. Identify publicly available business contact channels.
5. Record sources and verification dates.
6. Classify each company as a potential prospect unless actual purchasing activity is independently confirmed.
7. Assess import regulations, labeling requirements, and applicable trade restrictions before commercial outreach.

Do not invent company names, contact details, purchasing activity, or commercial relationships.
