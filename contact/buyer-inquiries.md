# International Buyer Inquiries

Tangsir Daryanavard Co. welcomes inquiries from food manufacturers, importers, distributors, food ingredient processors, and other qualified B2B buyers seeking dates and date ingredients for industrial applications.

## Products Available for Sourcing Inquiries

International buyers may submit inquiries regarding:

- Bulk Dates
- Industrial Date Paste
- Date Syrup for Food Manufacturing
- Date Sugar for Food Industry

Product availability, specifications, packaging, pricing, samples, lead times, and commercial terms must be confirmed for each inquiry.

## Inquiry Options

### Bulk Product Quotations

Request a commercial quotation for bulk dates or date ingredients.

Please include:

- Product required
- Intended industrial application
- Estimated order quantity
- Preferred packaging format
- Destination country and port
- Required delivery period
- Applicable technical or regulatory requirements

[REQUEST A QUOTATION](mailto:info@tangsirco.com?subject=Bulk%20Product%20Quotation%20Request)

### Technical Product Specifications

Buyers may request available technical documentation for product evaluation.

Depending on the product and supplier, buyers should request confirmation of:

- Product composition
- Ingredient declaration
- Moisture or solids information
- Texture and consistency
- Colour and flavour profile
- Microbiological parameters
- Allergen statement
- Storage conditions
- Shelf life
- Packaging options
- Country of origin
- Available quality and compliance documentation

No technical specification, certification, or compliance status should be assumed until it has been provided and verified for the specific product.

[REQUEST PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS](mailto:info@tangsirco.com?subject=Technical%20Product%20Specification%20Request)

### Product Samples

Food manufacturers may request samples for internal evaluation, laboratory review, formulation trials, or production testing.

Sample availability, quantity, shipping arrangements, documentation, and related costs are subject to confirmation.

[REQUEST A PRODUCT SAMPLE](mailto:info@tangsirco.com?subject=Product%20Sample%20Request)

To help us review your request efficiently, please provide:

| Inquiry Detail | Information Requested |
